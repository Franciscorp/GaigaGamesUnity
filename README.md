# Mysteries of Speech at Grandma's House Prototype
**Mysteries of Speech at Grandma's House** is an educational game developed as part of my master's thesis, exploring how digital games can support speech therapy for school-aged children who stutter.

Inspired by **GAIGA**, an intervention approach developed by the University of Aveiro, the project aims to transform traditional speech therapy activities into interactive and engaging gameplay experiences. Through storytelling, exploration, and minigames, players learn about speech, stuttering, and the mechanisms behind verbal communication in a welcoming and positive environment.

## Story
The player visits their grandmother's house, where they embark on an educational adventure together. Throughout the experience, the grandmother introduces different activities and challenges designed to help the player understand speech and become more familiar and comfortable with stuttering.

The game's narrative provides a friendly and supportive context in which children can explore these topics without pressure or fear of making mistakes.

## Minigames
### 1. Identifying Stuttering
Through an interactive activity with their grandparent, players learn to recognize the different types of stuttering, including repetitions, prolongations, and blocks.

The minigame uses examples with voice acting and gameplay choices to help children become more familiar with these speech patterns, encouraging understanding and reducing negative perceptions of stuttering.

### 2. The Talking Mannequin
A mannequin has mysteriously lost its ability to speak! To help it recover its voice, players must assemble the different components involved in human speech.

By identifying and assembling these components, players discover how speech production works, understand the role of each element, and explore how disruptions in the speech process can contribute to stuttering.

## Project Goals
- Explore the use of digital games as complementary tools for speech therapy.
- Promote awareness and understanding of stuttering.
- Encourage positive attitudes towards speech differences.
- Provide interactive and engaging learning experiences through storytelling and gameplay.

## Development
The game was developed iteratively in collaboration with speech therapy specialists, with the goal of creating an experience suitable for therapeutic environments and beyond.

Initial playtesting indicated that the narrative and interactive environments were engaging for children, highlighting the potential of digital games as complementary tools for speech therapy.

**This project serves as a proof of concept for using game development to create more interactive, accessible, and engaging speech therapy experiences.**

## Audio and Assets
As this project is a **proof of concept**, temporary assets were used throughout development to support prototyping and gameplay implementation. These assets may be replaced with final artwork and resources in future development stages.

**FMOD** was integrated to enhance the audio experience, providing greater control over sound implementation and helping improve audio quality and feedback during gameplay.

## Game Controls
- **Left / Right Arrows:** Move left and right.
- **Up / Down Arrows:** Move up and down.
- **Mouse:** Interact with the game and select choices.
